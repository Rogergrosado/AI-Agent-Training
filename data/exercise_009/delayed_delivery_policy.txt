POLICY ID: DEL-002
TITLE: Delayed Delivery Management
CATEGORY: Transportation Exceptions

A shipment is considered delayed when its expected delivery time has passed without confirmed delivery.

The logistics coordinator must verify the latest carrier tracking information and identify the reason for the delay.

The customer must receive an updated delivery estimate when reliable information becomes available.

If the delay affects a critical delivery commitment, the case must be escalated to the operations supervisor.

All communication, tracking updates, and corrective actions must be recorded in the shipment history.
