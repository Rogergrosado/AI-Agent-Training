POLICY ID: ESC-003
TITLE: Shipment Exception Escalation
CATEGORY: Operational Escalation

Shipment exceptions must be classified according to their operational impact and urgency.

Low-impact exceptions may be handled by the logistics coordinator following standard procedures.

Exceptions involving damaged goods, repeated delivery failures, missing shipments, or critical customer commitments require supervisor review.

The escalation record must include the shipment identifier, issue summary, supporting evidence, and actions already attempted.

The supervisor determines the appropriate corrective action and documents the final decision.

An exception must remain open until the resolution has been recorded and communicated to the relevant parties.
