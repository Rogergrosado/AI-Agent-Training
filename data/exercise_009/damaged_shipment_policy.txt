POLICY ID: DAM-001
TITLE: Damaged Shipment Handling
CATEGORY: Shipment Exceptions

If a shipment arrives with visible packaging damage, the receiving team must photograph the package before opening it.

The team must inspect the contents, record the shipment identifier, and document the condition of the goods.

If the goods are damaged, the shipment must be placed on hold and reported to the logistics supervisor.

A damage claim must include photographs, the shipment identifier, a description of the damage, and the receiving date.

The shipment must not be released for normal distribution until the responsible team authorizes the next action.
